Business Administration Skills
Management & leadership – planning, organizing, delegating, decision-making
Financial literacy – budgeting, accounting basics, financial analysis
Marketing – market research, branding, consumer behavior
Strategic thinking – business planning, competitive analysis
Operations management – process improvement, supply chain basics
Communication – business writing, presentations, negotiation
Human resources – team management, organizational behavior
Project management – timelines, budgets, resource allocation

Database management – SQL, data modeling, database design
Systems analysis & design – understanding business needs, translating them into technical requirements
Programming basics – often Python, Java, or similar (varies by program)
Business process modeling – flowcharts, UML, workflow analysis
Enterprise software – ERP systems (like SAP), CRM tools
Data analytics – Excel, BI tools (Power BI, Tableau), basic statistics
IT project management – Agile/Scrum methodologies
Cybersecurity fundamentals – basic risk and data protection awareness
Cloud computing basics – understanding of cloud services and infrastructure
